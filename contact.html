Get in Touch with TyagiHub

Communication is the lifeblood of any thriving educational community. At TyagiHub, we deeply value the feedback, technical inquiries, and profound insights of our global readership. Whether you are a first-year university student struggling to grasp the complexities of a Relational Database, a senior backend developer wanting to point out a highly specific optimization in our code, or a multinational organization looking to collaborate on educational initiatives, our inbox is always open and monitored.

1. Primary Contact Channel and Email Protocols

To ensure your message bypasses spam filters, reaches the correct department, and is processed as efficiently as possible, we mandate that all primary, serious communication be conducted via our official email channels. This allows us to track inquiries, forward highly technical questions to the appropriate author, and maintain a secure, written record of our correspondence.

Official Correspondence Email

Email: tyagisupport@gmail.com

2. Departmental Routing Guidelines

We receive a remarkably high volume of emails daily from students, educators, and professionals across the globe. To manage expectations and ensure a rapid response, please review our standard response protocols and use the appropriate subject lines when drafting your email:

  • General Inquiries & Reader Feedback (Subject: "Reader Feedback"): We absolutely love hearing how our massive guides have helped you ace a university exam, nail a technical interview, or finally understand a complex topic like Artificial Intelligence. While our editorial team reads every single piece of feedback, the sheer volume prevents us from replying to all of them personally. Please know your words motivate us daily.
  • Technical Corrections & Bug Reports (Subject: "Correction: [Article Title]"): Technology changes rapidly. If you spot a factual error, a typo, a broken hyperlink, or an outdated piece of code in one of our guides, please alert us immediately. We take our technical accuracy very seriously. Our engineering team will review the claim and, if verified, patch the error on the live site within 48 hours. We highly appreciate community QA!
  • Business, Sponsorship, & Partnership Inquiries (Subject: "Business Inquiry"): For serious sponsorship opportunities, ethical advertising placements, or institutional partnerships (e.g., universities wanting to use our content in their curriculum), please direct your email to our management team. Include a detailed proposal. We typically respond to serious, well-formatted business proposals within 2-3 business days.
  • DMCA and Legal Takedown Requests (Subject: "LEGAL: DMCA Request"): TyagiHub respects the intellectual property rights of all creators. If you believe your copyrighted work has been improperly used or hosted on our servers without fair use justification, please email our legal department with a formal DMCA notice including the exact URL and proof of ownership. We comply rapidly with all legitimate legal requests.

3. Guest Posting, SEO Backlinks, and Contribution Policy

We receive dozens of emails daily asking for "guest posting opportunities" or "SEO link insertions." Please read carefully: At this time, TyagiHub's content is exclusively authored by our rigorous in-house editorial team. This strict policy is necessary to maintain our unyielding quality standards, the specific "Mega-Guide" formatting, and the pedagogical consistency our readers expect. We are not currently accepting unsolicited guest posts. Any automated emails requesting SEO backlinks, paid link placements, or low-quality guest posts will be automatically filtered, marked as spam, and permanently ignored.

4. Security Vulnerability Reporting (Bug Bounty Info)

As a platform that teaches cybersecurity, we take our own infrastructure security very seriously. If you are a security researcher, ethical hacker, or penetration tester and you believe you have discovered a vulnerability in the TyagiHub architecture (e.g., XSS, CSRF, server misconfigurations), we urge you to report it to us immediately using the subject line "URGENT: Security Vulnerability." Please do not publicly disclose the vulnerability until we have had reasonable time to patch it. While we do not currently operate a paid bug bounty program, we will happily provide a letter of recommendation and a public acknowledgment on our site for any verified, critical disclosures.

5. Connect With Us on Social Media

If you prefer faster, more casual interaction, or if you simply want to stay updated the very second we drop a new 2,000-word tech guide, you can follow TyagiHub and its founder across various social media platforms. We frequently post bite-sized tech facts, industry updates, and behind-the-scenes looks at our writing process.

  • Twitter / X: Follow our official handle for rapid-fire tech insights, industry commentary, and immediate updates on site maintenance.
  • LinkedIn: Connect with our founder, Himanshu Tyagi, for professional networking, career-focused tech discussions, and deep-dive architectural posts.
  • GitHub: Check out our open-source repositories, where we host the complete, executable versions of the code snippets used in our various tutorials.

6. Office Hours, Availability, and Physical Location

TyagiHub operates as a decentralized, digital-first organization. Our core editorial and engineering team operates on Indian Standard Time (IST), headquartered in the National Capital Region (NCR), India. While our globally distributed servers provide knowledge 24/7/365, our human support team generally processes emails and inquiries between 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM (IST), Monday through Friday. We are closed on major national holidays. We appreciate your patience, respect your inquiries, and look forward to connecting with you as we build the ultimate digital literacy platform.
