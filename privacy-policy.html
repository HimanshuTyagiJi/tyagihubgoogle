Comprehensive Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 13, 2026

Welcome to TyagiHub, accessible from https://tyagihub.in. The privacy, security, and integrity of our visitors' personal data are of the utmost importance to us. This Comprehensive Privacy Policy details exactly how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information when you interact with our massive library of educational computer science resources. By accessing or utilizing TyagiHub, you explicitly consent to the data collection and usage practices described in this document. We adhere strictly to international data protection frameworks, including but not limited to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), and the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA).

1. Information We Collect and Receive

To provide a robust, fast, and secure educational platform, we must collect certain types of data. We adhere to the principle of data minimization—meaning we only collect what is absolutely necessary for the functioning of our services.

1.1 Automatically Collected Information (Log Data)

When you visit TyagiHub, our hosting servers automatically record standard log data. This is a fundamental operation of the internet and applies to nearly all websites globally. This log data includes your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, operating system details, the referring website that led you to our platform, the specific pages of TyagiHub that you visit, the time and date of your visit, and the time spent on those pages. This data is entirely anonymized and aggregated; it is impossible for us to trace this back to a specific individual identity.

1.2 Device and Diagnostic Information

We collect hardware and software diagnostic data to ensure our website renders correctly across all devices. This includes screen resolution, device manufacturer, mobile network information, and crash reports. If you experience a bug while reading one of our mega-guides, this diagnostic data allows our engineering team to patch the issue quickly.

1.3 Voluntarily Provided Information

While the vast majority of our content is available without registration, certain features require you to voluntarily submit personal data. If you contact us via email for support, subscribe to a future newsletter, or participate in community forums, you may provide us with your name, email address, academic affiliation, and the contents of your message. We treat this data with the highest level of confidentiality.

2. Cookies, Web Beacons, and Tracking Technologies

TyagiHub utilizes cookies, web beacons, pixels, and similar tracking technologies to enhance user experience and serve relevant advertisements. A cookie is a tiny, encrypted text file stored on your computer's hard drive by your web browser.

2.1 Essential Operations Cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary for the website to function. They handle load balancing, security protocols, and session management. You cannot opt-out of these cookies as the site would physically break without them.

2.2 Analytics and Performance Cookies

We use third-party analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to measure traffic. These cookies track how many users are reading our cybersecurity guides versus our artificial intelligence guides, helping us prioritize future content creation.

2.3 Google DoubleClick DART Cookie and AdSense Monetization

As a free educational resource, TyagiHub relies entirely on advertising revenue provided by Google AdSense to pay for server costs. Google, acting as a third-party vendor, uses DART cookies to serve ads to our users based on their browsing history on TyagiHub and other sites across the internet. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your digital footprint. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings at https://adssettings.google.com/. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. How We Utilize Your Data

The data we collect is strictly used for the following operational, security, and administrative purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To operate, maintain, and optimize the TyagiHub platform across a global Content Delivery Network (CDN).
  • Security and Fraud Prevention: To detect, investigate, and prevent malicious activities, including Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks, automated bot scraping, and unauthorized server access attempts.
  • Customer Support: To directly address any technical issues, educational inquiries, or legal requests submitted via our contact channels.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable international laws, court orders, or government requests, including tax and accounting regulations.

4. Third-Party Data Sharing and Disclosure

We have a strict anti-sale policy. TyagiHub does not, and will never, sell your personal data to data brokers or marketing agencies. However, we do share anonymized data with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website. These include cloud hosting providers, analytics services, and advertising networks (like Google AdSense). All third-party providers are legally bound by strict data processing agreements and are prohibited from utilizing your data for anything other than providing services to TyagiHub.

5. Comprehensive Global Privacy Rights

Depending on your geographic location, you are granted extensive rights regarding your personal data. We honor these rights globally, regardless of your citizenship.

5.1 General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) - European Union

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you possess the following rights: The Right to Access (requesting copies of your data), The Right to Rectification (correcting inaccurate data), The Right to Erasure (the "Right to be Forgotten" - requesting permanent deletion of your data), The Right to Restrict Processing, The Right to Object to Processing, and The Right to Data Portability. To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer.

5.2 California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA & CPRA)

If you are a resident of California, you have the right to request disclosure of the specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you over the past 12 months. You have the right to request deletion of your data and the right to opt-out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (though, again, we do not sell your data). We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.

5.3 LGPD (Brazil) and PIPEDA (Canada)

We fully comply with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law and the Canadian Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, ensuring lawful basis for processing, data transparency, and rapid breach notification protocols.

6. Data Security and Breach Protocols

We implement military-grade encryption (TLS 1.3) for all data transmitted between your browser and our servers. Our databases are secured behind strict firewalls and access-control lists (ACLs). In the highly unlikely event of a catastrophic data breach that compromises personally identifiable information, we are legally committed to notifying all affected users and the relevant data protection authorities within 72 hours of discovering the breach.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting the digital privacy of children is of paramount importance. TyagiHub provides advanced, university-level technical content. Our website is strictly not intended for individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personally identifiable information from children. If a parent or guardian believes that TyagiHub has inadvertently collected data from a child under 13, please contact us immediately so we can purge the information from our databases.

8. International Data Transfers

Because TyagiHub utilizes a globally distributed cloud infrastructure, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in countries outside of your country of residence (such as the United States). These countries may have data protection laws that differ from your local jurisdiction. By using our platform, you explicitly consent to these international data transfers.

9. Policy Updates and Contact Information

The digital landscape evolves daily, and our privacy practices must adapt accordingly. We reserve the right to amend, update, or overhaul this Privacy Policy at any time without prior individual notice. All modifications will be immediately published on this URL with an updated "Effective Date" at the top of the page. Your continued use of the website following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy. For any legal inquiries, data deletion requests, or questions regarding this document, please contact us at tyagisupport@gmail.com.
